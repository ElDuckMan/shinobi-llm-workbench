Build a tokenizer from scratch

Models don’t read characters or words — they read tokens. Byte-pair encoding builds a vocabulary by repeatedly merging the most frequent adjacent pair. Build it, then tokenize your own text.

Tokenize text

Apply the learned merges to any text. Common chunks become one token; rare text stays split into many.

GPT-4-class models use this exact algorithm with roughly 100,000 merges, trained on a large slice of the internet. Same idea, more scale.

This is a real neural network with a hidden layer. It reads the last few characters and learns, by gradient descent, to predict the next one. Nothing is hand-coded — it starts random and improves. Watch the numbers move.

Goal: train until the training loss drops below 2.10, then make it write a believable name.
